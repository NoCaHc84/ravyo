Getting started

  1. Open Ravyo and fill in Username, Password and Server URL with the details your provider gave you.
  2. Select Add user.
  3. Want to try it first? Select Try without an account (demo mode).

Using the remote

  • Arrows and OK: move and select.
  • OK on a channel: shows it in the preview; OK again for full screen.
  • Hold OK or press the yellow key: add to / remove from favorites.
  • In the player: up/down change channel; left/right skip 10 seconds back/forward. Controls hide by themselves after 5 seconds.
  • Back: returns to the previous screen.

FAQ

Does Ravyo include channels or movies?
No. Ravyo is only a player; content comes from your provider.

A channel cuts out or won't load.
The app retries by itself. If it persists, try Settings → Live TV format with HLS or MPEG-TS. If neither works, contact your provider.

How do I lock adult content?
Create a 4-digit PIN in Settings → Parental control.

How do I delete my data?
In Settings you can log out, clear favorites, clear "Continue watching" and clear the cache. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.
