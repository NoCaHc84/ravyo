Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the Ravyo application ("the app") uses and how. In short: the app has no accounts of its own, does not send your data to Ravyo servers, shows no advertising and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

1. Information stored on your TV

To work, the app stores the following locally, only in your TV's storage:

  • The login details you enter for your own provider (server address, username and password), so you don't have to type them every time.
  • Your favorites, recently viewed items and the position reached in each movie or episode ("Continue watching").
  • Your settings (for example, parental control). The parental PIN is stored hashed, never in plain text.

This information never leaves your TV towards Ravyo. You can delete it at any time from Settings (log out, clear favorites, clear "Continue watching", clear cache) or by uninstalling the app.

2. Connections the app makes

  • To your provider's server: the app connects directly to the server you enter to fetch channel, movie and series lists, the guide and the video streams. That provider receives your IP address and login details, as with any player, and handles them under its own privacy policy. Ravyo neither controls nor receives that data.
  • In demo mode: the app plays public test videos from third-party servers (Unified Streaming, Apple, Mux and the Internet Archive). Those services may log your IP address under their own policies.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not create user accounts on Ravyo servers.
  • It does not collect personal, usage or diagnostic data.
  • It does not show advertising or share information with advertisers.
  • It does not sell or transfer information to third parties.

4. Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. It includes a PIN-based parental control to restrict adult content categories.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date.

6. Contact

For any privacy question, write to ravyosoporte@gmail.com.
